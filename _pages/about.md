Hello! I'm a Research Scientist at [ASAPP](https://www.asapp.com/ai-research/) working on task-oriented dialog and natural language processing for conversational AI systems. I did my Ph.D. in Robotics at Carnegie Mellon University, where my research was generously supported by [Meta AI](https://ai.facebook.com/blog/facebook-ai-and-facebook-reality-labs-announce-research-and-mentorship-program-with-carnegie-mellon-university/) and [CMU Presidential Fellowship](https://www.cmu.edu/homepage/society/2014/spring/funding-the-best-and-the-brightest.shtml). 

My research focuses on building autonomous agents proficient in understanding language and performing actions. I'm passionate about developing products that help free up humans from mundane tasks, allowing them to be more creative and make better decisions.

In my current role, I work on building [multimodal agent models](https://www.asapp.com/blog/generative-ai-for-agent-augmentation-agents-models-not-language-models) that can handle conversational texts, action data streams, and business logic to augment human agents in customer service.
